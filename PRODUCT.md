# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, equal weight:

- **Business owners and teams** (marketing agencies, retail, sales operations) who need a system, an integration, or an AI automation built. They arrive from WhatsApp, Instagram, or a referral, often on a phone, and decide whether to start a conversation.
- **Recruiters and tech leads** evaluating Octávio for a role. They arrive from LinkedIn or GitHub, usually on desktop, and want proof of shipped work, stack, and education fast.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Octávio Augusto, full stack developer in Belo Horizonte (MG). It exists to turn a visit into a conversation: WhatsApp for clients, LinkedIn/e-mail for recruiters. Success means a visitor leaves knowing who he is (face included), what he has shipped in production, and how to reach him.

## Positioning

He builds systems that run at real clients, end to end: a self-checkout kiosk in production at Livraria Leitura (60–70% less checkout time), an AI voice receptionist, WhatsApp + AI funnels and a multi-niche CRM in production. Interface, integrations, infrastructure, and delivery by one person.

## Operating Context

- React 18 + Vite SPA, React Router, deployed to GitHub Pages (`dist/404.html` copy for SPA routing).
- Project detail pages at `/project-<id>` driven by `src/data/projects.js`.
- Profile, links, stack groups in `src/data/profile.js`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content is Portuguese (pt-BR).
- Several projects are under NDA: no source, no client screenshots beyond what exists in `public/assets/img/projects/`.
- Static hosting only; no backend, no forms that need a server.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Octávio Augusto. Handle: octaviocrv.
- Structural reference the user liked: brenoaguiar.com.br (`lpReferencia/`): a story told in numbered stages, featured work shown big, a "who builds this" section with a real photo, and a strong closing CTA. **Only the structure is binding**; its dark ground + single orange accent look is not to be copied.
- **Standing preference (2026-11-05):** after two direction rounds the user chose the category standard on purpose: a clean, conventional developer portfolio executed at full craft, no thematic concept. Quality bar: Stripe (precise type, clear hierarchy, numbers and proof shown well, generous space).
- The user explicitly wants to lose the "AI-made" look of the previous version (dark navy grid, neon accent, HUD chrome, mono labels everywhere).

## Product Principles

1. Show the person: his face and real working space carry trust that copy cannot.
2. Prove with shipped work, not adjectives: production status, client, measured result.
3. Two doors, one page: a client and a recruiter each find their next step without hunting.
4. Plain Portuguese, first person, no buzzword stacking.
